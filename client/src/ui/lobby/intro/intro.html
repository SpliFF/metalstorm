The World

01 / 03 · The World

One World. Held by whoever shows up.

A single persistent map, shared by everyone who plays. Factions hold ground, accrue what it yields, and stage attacks that take days to arrive — the attacker's days in transit are the defender's hours of warning.

Nothing here resets. Every Mission you fight is a piece of it: a region taken, a convoy delivered, a truce brokered. The World remembers.

Your Faction

02 / 03 · Your Faction

Your Faction

The people you fight for. Every Mission's sides are which Faction each team fights for; yours is chosen once and is the same in every one.

The Faction owns the army. Squads are lent to you, and what you are shown grows with your Standing.

Your Role

03 / 03 · Your Role

Recruit.

You will be given a few squads and a task. Do it well and the Faction gives you more. Watch, ask, and listen to a mentor if one offers — nobody expects a Recruit to win the Mission alone.
