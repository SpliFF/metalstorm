# Metalstorm art style bible

Authored 2026-07-09 (PLAN-metalstorm-beta-units.md, Concrete task 1). Everything
downstream — Option A/B sourcing (§3/§4), the normalisation script (§6) — keys off
this page + the class-scale table below.

## Silhouette-first, flat-shaded low-poly

- **Read at strategic zoom, not up close.** Most on-screen time is spent as a camera-
  facing impostor quad (§2.1's impostor ladder), not the 3D model. Silhouette and flat
  colour blocking carry the read; surface detail does not survive the distance.
- **Flat shading, no baked lighting.** No baked AO, no hand-painted grime/gradients.
  Colour comes from the shared palette atlas (below), assigned per-face/per-material.
  Team colour is a *separate* runtime mask — never baked into diffuse (engine path:
  `SPRINGRTS_team_color` glTF material extension, R channel of `maskTexture`,
  `client/src/core/entity-renderer.ts`).
- **Bevel rule:** every hard edge gets a small bevel (2 segments, ~2–4% of the piece's
  smallest dimension) so edges catch light without normal-map detail. Skip the bevel
  entirely on edges too small for the tri budget rather than adding a sliver.
- **Greeble budget: functional only.** Vents, hatches, antennae, weapon mounts — never
  decorative dressing. Nothing smaller than ~0.3 m real-world (invisible past gameplay
  zoom, wasted tris). Test: if it doesn't change the silhouette or read as a functional
  part from ~15 m, cut it.
- **Kinetic, not clean-sci-fi.** Weathering is a palette-swatch choice (a "worn steel"
  swatch, not a dirt gradient); weapons show exposed mechanical form (autocannon
  receivers, howitzer breeches) so they read as guns, matching PLAN-metalstorm.md §6
  ("explosive/projectile weapons dominate; lasers are rare").

## Shared palette atlas

`unittextures/atlas_palette.ktx2` — one flat-colour swatch sheet, 4×4 grid of 64 px
cells (256×256 source), generated by `tools/scripts/make_palette_atlas.py` (the PNG
source is never committed — `unittextures/README.md`'s ".ktx2 only" rule — regenerate
from the script + `toktx` whenever a swatch changes). Every normalised model UVs its
flat-shaded faces onto a swatch cell — any pixel within a cell is correct, so UV
placement doesn't need to be pixel-precise.

| Row | Swatches (col 0→3) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | hull light · hull mid · hull dark · armor plate | primary structural colour; 4-step value ramp doubles as cheap AO-by-swatch |
| 1 | neutral accent/trim · hazard yellow · worn steel · rust brown | detail call-outs, wear |
| 2 | canopy/glass blue · emissive cyan (engine glow) · exhaust orange · muzzle-flash white | emissive/transparent bits, unlit in-shader |
| 3 | concrete grey · building steel · civilian tan · ground-contact dark | buildings & civilian props |

## Class-scale table (real-world metres)

Two cross-checked sources, both already load-bearing elsewhere — this table doesn't
invent new physics, it reconciles them for artists:

- **Sim footprint** (already authored in `units/_builder.lua`, read not re-derived):
  `footprintx = baseFootprint + (scale-1)`; metres = `footprintx × SPRING_FOOTPRINT_SCALE(2)
  × SQUARE_SIZE(8) × ELMOS_TO_METERS(1/8)` = **`footprintx × 2`**
  (`rts/Sim/Misc/GlobalConstants.h`). For squads (squad_size > 1) this is the
  order/formation *marker* box, not a per-member bound — members fan out over
  `formation_radius` (a separate, larger value). For single-entity classes (radar,
  static defense, ships, subs, scale-4, buildings) the model should roughly fill it,
  though — same as classic Spring games — a model may overhang a square footprint cell
  (gun barrels, wings); footprint is a movement-grid abstraction, not a tight AABB.
- **Real-world military analogue**, scaled scout→MBT→heavy→dreadnought per the
  flavour text, gives the *individual model's* dominant dimension (length for
  ground/naval, wingspan for air, height for bipeds). This is what
  `tools/scripts/normalize_model.py`'s `--dominant-dim`/`--target-metres`
  arguments target.

| Class | Dominant dim | s1 light/swarm | s2 line | s3 heavy | s4 super-heavy | Footprint check (m) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| soldiers / engineers | height (human-scale — armour bulk grows, not literal height) | 1.8 m | 1.85 m | 1.9 m | 2.1 m (powered rig) | 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 (formation box, not member size) |
| tanks | length | 4.5 m (tankette) | 8.5 m (MBT) | 12 m (heavy platform) | 26 m (dreadnought) | 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 |
| artillery | length | 4.5 m | 7.5 m | 10.5 m | 15 m | 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 |
| mechs | height (biped/quad) | 3 m | 5 m | 7.5 m | 11 m | 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 |
| fighters | wingspan | 6 m | 9 m | 12 m | 16 m | 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 |
| bombers | wingspan | 8 m | 12 m | 16 m | 22 m | 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 |
| ships | length | 20 m | 35 m | 55 m | 80 m | 8 / 10 / 12 / 14 |
| subs | length | 18 m | 30 m | 45 m | 65 m | 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 |
| static defense | height (emplacement) | 3 m | 4.5 m | 6 m | 8 m | 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 |
| radar | height (mast+dish) | 4 m | 6 m | 8 m | 11 m | 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 |

Not part of the 4-scale system (`units/civilians.lua`, `units/civvehicles.lua`,
`units/buildings_*.lua` — flat defs, no `_builder.lua`):

| Def | Dominant dim | Metres | Footprint check |
|---|---|---|---|
| civilian person (`ms_civilians`/`ms_militia`) | height | 1.8 m (impostor-first, §2.1) | 4 × 4 |
| civilian truck (`ms_civtruck`) | length | 6 m | 4 × 6 |
| civilian bus (`ms_civbus`) | length | 10 m | 4 × 6 (model overhangs — footprint abstraction, see above) |
| nexus/HQ (`ms_command_nexus`) | footprint edge | 24 × 24 m | 12 × 12 cells |
| foundry (`ms_foundry`) | footprint edge | 32 × 40 m | 16 × 20 cells |
| habitat (`ms_habitat`) | footprint edge | 24 × 24 m | 12 × 12 cells |
| transit hub (`ms_transit_hub`) | footprint edge | 20 × 28 m | 10 × 14 cells |

Building metres = `footprintx/z × 2`, read directly from `units/buildings_*.lua`.

## Tri budgets (§2, restated for the normalisation harness)

Infantry/civilian-person ≤ 800 tri *if modelled at all* (impostor-first is the beta
default, §2.1 — sprite atlas, no 3D model required); vehicles (tanks, artillery,
fighters, bombers, mechs, static defense, radar, civilian truck/bus) ≤ 2,000 tri;
scale-4 hero ≤ 8,000 tri. Buildings are unbudgeted here — footprint-driven, not
tri-driven; flat shading keeps them cheap by construction.
