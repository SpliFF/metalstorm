ms_dress_resistance — Resistance dressing kit (accessory set)
=============================================================

Five ROOT pieces in one glTF: net, stow, rack, flag, smoke.
Each piece is authored with its LOCAL origin at the mount plane (y=0 =
deck/attach surface), forward -Z, 1 unit = 1 m.

The root offsets baked into the file (net 0 / stow -6 / rack -9 / flag +6 /
smoke +9 on X) are a DISPLAY FAN-OUT only, so the pieces do not overlap when
the whole model is previewed. Integrators placing individual pieces must
ZERO the root offset and use the mount offsets below. NOTE: the engine
radius is computed over the fanned-out kit — derive combat/selection radii
per placed piece, not from this file's bounds.

Mount offsets (hull frames; from toolkit layout.py / heavy_layout.py)
