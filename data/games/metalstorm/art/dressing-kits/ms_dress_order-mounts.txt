ms_dress_order — Order dressing kit: intended mount offsets
============================================================

Frame: RH, forward = -Z, up = +Y, metres. All kit elements are ROOT
pieces authored resting on Y=0 with display fan-out root offsets
(applique -4.2 / staff -1.6 / lightbar +0.9 / stowage +3.4 along X).
ZERO the root offset when attaching, then use the offsets below.

Reference hull dims (toolkit layout.py / heavy_layout.py):
  fable_tank : half-width 1.75, hull z -4.40..4.40, deck y 1.86,
               hull side y 0.15..2.05, turret ring (0, 1.80, 0.30),
               intake z 2.45, exhausts (±0.80, 1.60, 3.92)
  fable_heavy: half-width 2.35, hull z -8.10..8.10, deck y 3.02,
               hull side y 0.25..3.15, turret ring (0, 3.00, -0.60),
               exhaust z 7.30

applique (plates authored upright facing -Z; plate local centres:
side plate x=-1.55 c_y=0.50 [2.0x1.0x0.1], glacis x=+0.45 c_y=0.45
[1.5x0.9x0.1], ID/team panel x=+2.00 c_y=0.45 [1.1x0.9x0.1] — rebase
each plate to its own centre when mounting):
  side plate  — rotate 90° about Y, flush on hull side:
                tank  (±1.81, 1.30, -0.80)
                heavy (±2.41, 1.90, -1.60)
  glacis plate— tilt about X to glacis slope (~55° from vertical):
                tank  (0, 1.35, -3.60)
                heavy (0, 2.20, -6.90)
  ID panel    — hull rear, upright:
                tank  (0, 1.10, 4.46)
                heavy (0, 1.70, 8.16)

staff (base flange at piece origin; pennant tip reaches +0.98 local X,
staff top y 2.72):
  tank  rear-deck corner: (-1.45, 1.86, 3.90)
  heavy rear-deck corner: (-2.00, 3.02, 7.60)

lightbar (stub feet at y=0; bar centre y 0.62, lenses face -Z):
  tank  hull deck, behind turret: (0, 1.86, 2.05)
  heavy hull deck, behind turret: (0, 3.02, 3.40)
  (avoid tank intake at z 2.45 if raised mounts are added)

stowage (tray underside at y=0; footprint 1.70 x 0.72):

  heavy engine deck: (0, 3.02, 5.60)

about the staff axis, 2.4 s seamless loop.
Team colour: pennant field + ID applique panel (team mask R only).
