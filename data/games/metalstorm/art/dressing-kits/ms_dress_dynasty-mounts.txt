ms_dress_dynasty — Dynasty dressing kit (opulent-salvage: gold trim over
worn steel). Accessory ROOT pieces in one glTF; each piece is authored
about its own MOUNT point, so attach by placing the piece origin at the
hull socket below and ZERO the display fan-out offset baked into the file.

Frame: RH, -Z forward, +Y up, metres. Team colour: banner field only
(team mask R). Emissive: lantern panes, warm amber.

Pieces (root unless noted)
  banner      pole+crossbar+finial; mount plate bottom at local y=0
    flag      child of banner at (0, 2.28, 0); clip `idle` = 3.6 s
              seamless cloth sway about the crossbar X axis
  rail_l/_r   gilt trim rail, 3.72 m run along Z, stanchion feet y=0
  crest       plaque; BACK face is the mount plane at local z=+0.055,
              bottom edge y=0 (stand off a vertical hull face by 0.055)
  lantern_l/_r  carriage lantern, base plate bottom y=0, ~0.61 m tall
  cowl_l/_r   exhaust cowl, flange bottom y=0, mouth up, 0.76 m tall
