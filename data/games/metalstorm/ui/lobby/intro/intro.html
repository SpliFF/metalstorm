The World

01 / 03 · The World

The rift. Held by whoever shows up.

After the collapse, the only things worth fighting for were the things still running: foundries, rail, derricks, the survey maps. One persistent World holds all of it. Factions take ground, keep what it yields, and stage attacks that take days to arrive — a march is the defender's warning.

Nothing resets. Every Mission is a piece of the World: a region taken, a convoy delivered, a truce brokered. The World remembers.

Recruit.

You will be given a few squads and a task. Do it well and the Faction gives you more. Watch, ask, and take a mentor if one offers — nobody expects a Recruit to win the Mission alone, and the Faction does not hand its best armour to an unknown.
