# Metalstorm — Player Guide

Metalstorm is a team strategy game. The team owns the army; you lend it your judgement.
Objectives matter more than reflexes. Authority — the one resource — is earned by
completing objectives and spent giving orders.

## Getting started

- **Watch first.** Type a callsign on the welcome screen and press *Watch as*. No account,
  no password. You can join a Mission as a spectator and see how a battle breathes.
- **Sign up to play.** A callsign and a password. You pick a Faction once; it is your side
  in every Mission.
- **Your first Mission** is a small one. Play *Solo* for a private training Mission, or
  *Support* to join a larger battle in a minor role. Both count toward your Standing.

## The World

One persistent World, shared by everyone. Factions hold ground, accrue treasury and stage
attacks. The World runs on its own clock — one World day per real hour — and plays out over
days. *Explore* opens the World map from the hub: points of interest, who holds them, and
which Missions are open.

## Missions

A Mission is a bounded task on one map. It is not always a battle: reconnaissance, escort,
transport and diplomacy are Missions too. A Mission's sides are which Faction each team
fights for.

- **Support** — join a running Faction Mission. As a Recruit you are given a few squads and
  a task; the rest of the team carries the battle.
- **Solo** — your own short Mission with a capped roster. Training, at your pace.
- **Broadcast** — watch a Mission as it was, an hour behind, with no live intel leaking.

## Factions

Two battle Factions: **The Meridian Compact** (engineers and surveyors; builds first,
fights second) and **The Foundry Union** (foundry crews and shipyard guilds; hands on the
line). They are identity, not mechanics — the same units, different people.

## Standing

Standing is your rank inside the Faction. It grows with every completed Mission, every
objective you are credited on and every mentor endorsement.

| Tier | Name | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recruit | assigned squads, focus HUD, a mentor |
| 1 | Regular | command any unassigned squad, scoreboard, parley |
| 2 | Veteran | mentor recruits, suggest objectives, found a World faction |
| 3 | Officer | create Missions, AI command, Faction intelligence |
| 4 | Commander | delegation and standing orders |

Low Standing means little command and no inner secrets. The Faction does not hand its
best units to an unknown — earn them.

## Mentorship

Recruits are visible to their Faction. A Veteran may offer to mentor you; an AI mentor
offers if nobody does. Under mentorship the HUD quiets outside chatter so you can focus.
A mentor can order your squads and hand you tasks; the HUD shows both. Declining keeps
your independence within your rank.

## Orders and authority

- Left-click selects a squad; right-click orders it to move. Type or speak an order in the
  command bar — "hold the ridge", "scout north" — and the game interprets it against what
  you have selected.
- Every order costs authority. Micro-management costs double; postures and delegation are
  cheap. Fighting on ground your team holds is half price; on enemy ground, double.
- Objectives are the only income. Completing one pays the team; a chain offer follows a
  captured region at a bonus.

## Squads and force

The squad is the unit of command. Squads shrink as their scale grows: sixteen light
soldiers, one super-heavy flagship. Strength never regenerates — new force arrives only
by transport, and force leaves alive only by departure. Engineers build field works
(trenches, barricades, watchtowers), never factories.

## Reading the HUD

- **Authority bar** — what the team can afford right now.
- **Focus** — the squads you are responsible for, first.
- **Objectives** — what pays, and who is on it.
- **Moments** — what just happened that concerns you.

## Spectating

A spectator sees the whole map and hears every channel, but a Mission you watch live is
one you cannot be playing. Broadcasts are replays delayed by at least one hour, so nothing
you see can be fed back to a battle in progress.

## Tutorial

*Start the tutorial* runs a Solo Mission on the Scorched Crossing proving grounds: move a
column, take a region, hold it. A coach card walks you through each beat.
