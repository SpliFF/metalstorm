## What screenshot to find

An Impaler firing its artillery missile. Want the smoke-trailed missile climbing then arcing down onto the target.

**Search hints:** `Zero-K Impaler`, `Zero-K Impaler firing`, ZK wiki unit
page for **Impaler**, or the ZK in-game unit description ("Precision Artillery Rover").
