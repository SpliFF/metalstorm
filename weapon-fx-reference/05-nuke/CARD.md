## What screenshot to find

A Trinity nuke launch and/or the mushroom detonation. The big explosion CEG is the key thing to match.

**Search hints:** `Zero-K Trinity`, `Zero-K Trinity firing`, ZK wiki unit
page for **Trinity**, or the ZK in-game unit description ("Strategic Nuclear Launcher").
