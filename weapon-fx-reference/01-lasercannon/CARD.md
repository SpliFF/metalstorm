A Bandit firing its laser at a ground target. Look for the travelling coloured bolt with a glowing cap, not a continuous beam.

**Search hints:** `Zero-K Bandit`, `Zero-K Bandit firing`, ZK wiki unit
page for **Bandit**, or the ZK in-game unit description ("Medium-Light Raider Bot").
